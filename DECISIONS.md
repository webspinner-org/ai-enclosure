## 2026-05-09 — Project Inception

### Title and Subtitle
- **Title locked:** "AI Enclosure" (no leading "The").
- **Subtitle locked:** "Why Sovereign Intelligence Demands Warp Speed."
- The "AI" in the title is deliberate — meets readers in their existing vocabulary. Body text uses SI throughout.

### Author Byline
- "John D. Marx, Founder, The Webspinner Foundation."
- The Foundation framing is used for moral and movement claims throughout the book; Webspinner LLC and Webspinner Cloud are reserved for technical and commercial references.

### Terminology
- **Synthetic Intelligence (SI)** replaces Artificial Intelligence (AI) in body text. Rationale: "artificial" carries a quiet diminishment ("fake, imitation, lesser-than"); "synthetic" tells the truth — these systems are *made by synthesis*, real but constructed.
- **Sovereign Intelligence** chosen over "Sovereign AI" — the phrase names both human and synthetic sovereignty simultaneously, and avoids the nationalist capture of "Sovereign AI" (state-controlled AI) in policy discourse.
- **Hyperscalers / Hyperscale AI** is the standard term for the antagonist throughout.
- Webspinner's existing proprietary terms — Warp, Cell, Loom, Weaver, Grimoire, WRAG, BYOK — are retained as proper nouns and capitalized accordingly.

### Structure
- **Five-part structure**, 27 chapters, plus front matter and appendices.
- **Four pillars** of Warp: Green SI, Confidential SI, Sovereign SI, Moral AI. The Value Triangle (cost/speed/quality) is the proof of these pillars, not a fifth pillar.
- **Note on Terminology** placed before the Executive Summary as the framing device for the entire book.

### Voice and Posture
- Inventor's voice. Authority drawn from the author's PC-era track record (CGL, Open Systems Group, Inc. 500 #127 in 1996).
- Alarmist where stakes are real. The phrase "the largest single threat to it in human history" in the Note on Terminology is intentional and stays.
- Antagonist is structural (Hyperscale AI), not personal. Critique concentration and incentives, not individuals or specific companies.
