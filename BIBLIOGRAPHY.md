# Bibliography

A consolidated bibliography for *AI Enclosure*, organized by chapter. Footnote numbers in the chapter prose correspond to the numbered entries below. Each chapter's notes appear in order of citation.

This file is canonical for citations. When chapters are exported for book production, the inline footnote blocks at the end of each chapter file are stripped; this bibliography becomes the book's back matter.

When sources are added or revised, update them here as well as in the chapter file.

## Chapter 1 — The Hyperscale Trap

1. William Stanley Jevons, *The Coal Question: An Enquiry Concerning the Progress of the Nation, and the Probable Exhaustion of Our Coal-Mines* (London: Macmillan, 1865), Chapter VII, "Of the Economy of Fuel." The "Jevons paradox" was named in modern economics literature in the late twentieth century; the underlying argument is Jevons's. Full text via the Library of Economics and Liberty: https://www.econlib.org/library/YPDBooks/Jevons/jvnCQ.html

2. International Energy Agency, *Energy and AI* (Paris: IEA, April 2025). https://www.iea.org/reports/energy-and-ai. See also IEA, *Electricity 2024: Analysis and Forecast to 2026* (Paris: IEA, January 2024). The 2024 figure of approximately 415 TWh, the 2030 projection of approximately 945 TWh, and the thirty-percent annual growth rate for accelerated-server consumption are from *Energy and AI*.
