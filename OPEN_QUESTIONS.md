## Strategic / Editorial

- **Publishing path.** Self-publish via the Webspinner Foundation? Traditional publisher? Hybrid? Each option carries a different distribution strategy.
- **Companion materials.** Workbook? Video series? Short policy paper for legislative audiences? Talk circuit?
- **Outside foreword.** Should we invite a foreword from a respected outside voice (a PC-era figure, a technologist-turned-critic, an ethicist) in addition to the author's foreword?

## Structural

- **Cooperative Ethic placement.** Is Chapter 26 (The Cooperative Ethic) better placed before or after Chapter 25 (Webspinner's Role)? The current order argues role first, then ethic; reverse argues ethic frames the role.
- **Note on Terminology numbering.** Numbered front-matter section, or unnumbered preface? Affects table-of-contents readability.
- **Glossary placement.** Front matter (early reference for readers learning the vocabulary) or back matter (traditional position)?

## Content

- **Hyperscale AI energy and water figures.** Which sources do we anchor on for credibility? Need defensible, recent numbers — likely a mix of academic studies, NGO reports, and direct utility filings.
- **Documented examples in Chapter 22.** Which cases of centralized AI being used for surveillance, military contracting, or content moderation at scale are strongest and most defensible against legal pushback?
- **PC parallel depth in Chapter 24.** How heavily to lean on personal narrative (the author's lived experience) versus structural analysis? Likely hybrid; ratio TBD.
- **The Foundation pledge in Chapter 26.** The exact language of "things Warp will never be allowed to do, by design." Needs to be specific enough to be meaningful and general enough to survive future technical changes.

## Technical

- **Part III depth.** Engineering reader vs. policy reader balance. Risk: too technical alienates the policy/general audience; too shallow loses the engineers who would otherwise become Cell operators and contributors.
- **Diagrams.** Style direction, illustrator, file format. Probably want a consistent visual language across architecture diagrams and comparative figures.
- **Code samples.** Include any in the book, or relegate to a reference repo? Leaning toward reference repo to keep the book accessible.

## Movement

- **Cell operator covenant.** What does it mean to operate a Warp Cell in alignment with the Foundation's ethics? Voluntary code of conduct, signed pledge, or technical attestation?
- **Foundation governance.** Who decides what Warp is and isn't? Open question that the book may need to gesture toward without fully answering.
